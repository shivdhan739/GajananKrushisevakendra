1. Who we are

This website (“Krushi Shop”) is run by Gajanan Shivsamb Deshmukh, Shivam Krushi Kendra, Kawalgaon, Tq-Purna, Dist-Parbhani – 431511, Maharashtra, India. We sell seeds, fertilizers and other farm inputs. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Shivam Krushi Kendra, and “you” means a visitor or registered customer of this website.

2. Information we collect

When you register:

  • First name and last name, mobile number, email address
  • District, taluka and village / address
  • Land area and primary crops (optional farming details)
  • Password: it is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We cannot see or read your password.
  • Aadhaar number: you enter your 12-digit number so that it can be checked for validity. We store only the last 4 digits (shown masked as XXXX XXXX 1234). The full number is not saved in our database.

When you place an order:

  • Your name, mobile number, email, delivery / pickup choice and delivery address
  • The items, quantities and total amount, and the payment method you choose (cash or UPI)
  • For UPI payments, the transaction reference (UTR) you type, so that we can verify the payment
  • For products that need identity verification (such as fertilizers or pesticides), the last 4 digits of your Aadhaar, which are attached to the order and bill

When you log in: your name, email, and the date and time you logged in and out (used for security and account activity).

When you use the Contact form: your name, mobile number, email (if you give it), the subject and your message.

Technical and security data: to protect the website we use an image captcha, Google reCAPTCHA / Firebase App Check, and a counter of failed login attempts that is kept in your browser. Your browser keeps a temporary login session. Our hosting and service providers may also see technical data such as your IP address and browser type when you open the site.

What we do not collect: we do not collect your card or bank account details (UPI payments are made in your own UPI app), your precise location, or your contacts. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not run third-party analytics on this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and to prevent duplicate registrations
  • To process, pack, deliver and bill your orders, and to confirm payments
  • To send you OTPs, registration confirmations, order confirmations and bills by email
  • To answer your questions and contact-form messages
  • To keep the website safe, detect misuse and stop automated attacks
  • To keep records that the law may require for the sale of regulated farm inputs

We use your information only for these purposes, and we do not use it for advertising.

4. Your consent

By registering, placing an order or sending us a message, you agree to this policy and to us handling your information as described here. You can withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us (see section 10). If you withdraw consent, we may not be able to continue your account or process new orders.

5. Who we share information with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run this website, and only as needed:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore database, App Check): stores your account and order records and handles sign-in.
  • Google reCAPTCHA / Fraud Defense: checks that the visitor is not a bot.
  • Brevo (email delivery, through our own mail relay on Cloudflare Workers): sends OTP, confirmation and bill emails, and sends order and contact notifications to the shop.
  • Google Fonts and Cloudflare cdnjs: load the website fonts and the QR-code library. These providers receive your IP address when your browser loads them.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files.

The shop owner and authorised staff can view your registration, order and login records to run the business. We may also disclose information if required by law, a court order or a government authority.

6. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers and processed by the providers listed above. These servers may be located outside India. We choose well-known providers that use their own security measures to protect data.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your account information while your account is active. We keep order, bill and payment records for as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal requirements. After that, or when you ask us to delete your data (where the law allows), we delete or anonymise it.

8. How we protect your information

  • Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, and passwords are not visible to us
  • Database rules allow a customer to read only their own data; only the shop admin can see all records
  • Image captcha, App Check and a temporary lock after repeated wrong passwords protect against automated guessing
  • Only the last 4 digits of Aadhaar are kept

No website or online system can be completely secure. Please keep your password private, use a strong and unique password, and log out on shared devices.

9. Your rights

You may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you,
  • correct information that is wrong or incomplete,
  • delete your account and information (subject to records we must keep by law),
  • stop using your information for any purpose you no longer agree to.

You can reset a forgotten password yourself using “Forgot password” on the login page.

For any question, request or complaint about your information, please contact us. We will reply within a reasonable time.
